# Regulation 003 - Crypto-Asset Lending Guidelines

§2.1 Asset Verification
Lending against crypto-assets requires real-time proof of reserves. Platforms must audit their collateral pools every 24 hours.

§2.5 KYC Exemption (AMENDED)
For crypto-backed loans under $5,000, platforms are temporarily exempt from the strict reverification requirements outlined in REGULATION_001, provided the user has passed an initial AML (Anti-Money Laundering) screen.
